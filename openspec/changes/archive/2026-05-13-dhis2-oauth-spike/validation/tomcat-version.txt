CONCLUSION: Rfc6265CookieProcessor.sameSiteCookies IS available — it was
backported to Tomcat 8.5 in 8.5.47 (released 2019-05-08). We are running
8.5.88, so the attribute is reachable. However the filter-based approach in
dhis2-iframe-embedding D2 remains valid and is simpler to configure at
runtime. The Rfc6265CookieProcessor path is an option if the filter approach
proves problematic.
